THE QUESTIONS AN OWNER SHOULD ASK

Does this earn a place in my sales process?

RentSketch should complement the operation you already trust—not create another system your staff has to fight.

Does RentSketch replace my rental software?

No. RentSketch is the customer-facing visual planning and quote-request layer. Keep authoritative availability, reservations, contracts, payments, routing and warehouse operations in the system that runs those jobs.

Will it work with my equipment?

The catalog supports names, dimensions, pricing, product photos and supported visual mappings. Test your important products during the free trial; a custom 3D model for every possible item is not automatically included.

Why would this increase order value?

RentSketch makes optional rentals easier to see in context—such as linens, sidewalls, lighting, dance floors, cocktail tables or inflatables. That creates more opportunities for customers to discover and request add-ons, but actual sales lift should be measured from your own activity rather than assumed.

How do I know if customers actually use it?

Design Intelligence aggregates meaningful saved-layout behavior, quote-request conversion, repeated combinations and friction signals. As real activity accumulates, use those patterns to judge adoption and improve the experience.
