STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Know what RentSketch owns.

RentSketch is the visual planning layer. Equipment availability, installation approval and final booking remain with the rental business and its operational systems.

Is RentSketch for rental businesses or individual events?

Both. Rental companies get a branded designer, catalog and business dashboard. Someone planning a single event can use the separate one-time Event Pass.

What is Photo Match?

Photo Match lets a saved design use an uploaded backyard or venue photo as a calibrated planning reference. It helps place supported equipment against a real scene; it does not turn one photo into a guaranteed survey or engineering model.

Can I save different versions of a layout?

Yes. Saved projects support named checkpoints and alternative layouts so you can compare changes without overwriting the only copy of the plan.

Does RentSketch replace my booking or inventory system?

No. RentSketch focuses on visual customer planning, supported rental catalogs, saved layouts and quote requests. Keep authoritative availability, reservations, contracts, dispatch and accounting in the system that owns those workflows.

Can I put the designer on my rental website?

Yes. A business workspace can configure approved website domains and use a direct designer link or the supported embed flow.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The public demo is free. Business workspaces have a 14-day no-card trial. Individual planners can explore the demo before choosing the one-time Event Pass.
